# Learning Process

## 1. How to Learn Faster with the Feynman Technique

### Question 1
Pick a topic, explain it in simple words as if you are teaching a kid, and go back to study the parts where you got stuck.

## 2. Learning How to Learn TED talk by Barbara Oakley

### Question 2
What I liked most is that Barbara Oakley was bad at maths and science in school, but later she became an engineering professor. It made me feel that being weak at something today does not mean I will always be weak at it. She also explained that breaks, exercise, and good sleep help the brain learn better. I want to use this when I get stuck on a hard topic.

### Question 3
- Active mode: the brain is fully focused on a problem, like when I am solving a coding bug.
- Diffused mode: the brain is relaxed, like when I take a walk or rest, and it keeps thinking in the background.
- Hard problems get easier when I switch between both modes.
- If I am stuck, a short break often helps me find the answer.

## 3. Learn Anything in 20 hours

### Question 4
- Break the skill into small parts.
- Learn just enough to start practicing and fix my own mistakes.
- Remove distractions that stop me from practicing.
- Practice for at least 20 hours.

### Question 5
- Work on one thing at a time and keep my phone on silent.
- Explain every concept in my own words, as if I am teaching someone.
- Take short breaks so my mind can rest and think in the background.
- Write code and practice instead of only watching videos.
- Set a deadline for each topic and track my time.
- Ask my mentor for help early when I am stuck.
- Revise what I learned at the end of each day.
