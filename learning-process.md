# Learning Process

## 1. How to Learn Faster with the Feynman Technique

### Question 1
The Feynman Technique means learning a concept by explaining it in simple terms, as if you were teaching it to a beginner, then fixing any gaps you find.

## 3. Learning How to Learn TED talk by Barbara Oakley

### Question 2
Barbara Oakley struggled with mathematics and science in school, but she later became an engineering professor. This demonstrates that anyone can learn difficult subjects by using the right approach, which includes studying, taking breaks, and getting good sleep.

### Question 3

* Casual mindset: a relaxed state of mind, during times of rest or leisure.
* Complex problems are often solved through an exchange between two parties.

## 4. Learn Anything in 20 hours

### Question 4
* Break the skill down into smaller parts.
* Simply learn to train and correct yourself.
* Remove distractions and obstacles to work.
* Practice for 20 hours or more.

### Question 5
* Focus on one task, and set my phone to silent mode.
* Explain each concept in my own words, as if teaching someone else.
* Take short breaks so that my mind can rest and reflect in the background.
* Practice by writing code, not just by reading and watching.
* Set a deadline for each subject and track the time I spend on them.
* Tell my instructor immediately when I am ready.
