# Listening and Active Communication

## 1. Active Listening

### Question 1
- Give full attention to the speaker.
- Do not cut in while they talk.
- Say small things like "okay" or "I see" to show interest.
- Look at the speaker and nod.
- Take notes if the talk is important.
- Say the main point back in my own words to check I understood.

## 2. Reflective Listening

### Question 2
- Listen more and talk less.
- Pay attention to the person's feelings, not only the facts.
- Say back what I heard to check it is right.
- Do not judge or give advice too fast.
- Watch body language and tone, not only words.
- Keep away distractions.

## 3. Reflection

### Question 3
- My mind drifts to other work.
- I look at my phone when it buzzes.
- I plan my answer while the other person is still talking.
- Loud noise around me.

### Question 4
- Keep my phone on silent.
- Wait for the person to finish before I reply.
- Take notes in meetings.
- Repeat the key points back to the speaker.

## 4. Types of Communication

### Question 5
- When I do not want an argument, I stay quiet and say "okay".
- When I feel the other person will not listen anyway.

### Question 6
- When I am tired or stressed, I lose patience and speak loudly.
- When the same mistake happens again and again.

### Question 7
- When I am upset but do not want to say it, I give short answers or stay silent.
- Sometimes I make sarcastic jokes instead of saying what is bothering me.

### Question 8
- Say clearly what I need, in a calm voice.
- Use "I" sentences, like "I feel stressed when plans change late."
- Say no politely when I cannot do something.
- Keep a steady voice and open body
